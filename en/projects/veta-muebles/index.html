02Business website / Proposal demo

Veta Muebles

Catalog, configurator and quotes for a custom furniture workshop.

Freelance project · Fictional brand

Challenge
Quoting custom furniture means measuring, choosing materials and pricing every order by hand.
Result
Customers configure their piece, see an estimated price instantly and send the quote over WhatsApp.

About the project

Navigable demo built as a commercial proposal for a custom furniture workshop. It is shown under a fictional brand to protect the client's identity. It includes three versions of the site (Basic, Standard and Premium) and a demo management panel.

Problem
Quotes for custom furniture depend on measurements, materials and hardware, and are put together by hand for every inquiry.
Solution
A configurator estimates the price while the customer picks measurements and materials, adds several pieces to one quote and sends it over WhatsApp. The panel shows how the workshop would track requests, production, catalog and prices.

Technical approach

  • Pricing engine in TypeScript with no React dependencies: it estimates the cut list per furniture type (board, backs, edge banding, hardware and workshop hours) and validates measurements and combinations.
  • Persistent multi-product cart and a quote message ready for WhatsApp.
  • Three commercial plans defined in a single configuration; the router picks pages from each plan's features.
  • Management panel with sample data: overview, requests, formal quote, production, catalog, prices and customers. Changes are kept only in the browser session.
  • Vitest tests for the pricing engine, the cart and the WhatsApp message.
